A real New York kitchen, reconstructed from photos. You drive the robot through a pinhole; the camera you pick is previewed bottom right, through its lens, and that is what a capture records. Captures are listed at the bottom: click one to view it with its depth, download it or delete it.

W A S D
drive: forward, left, back, right (or hold the keys on screen)
Shift
drive faster
Mouse
turn the robot and tilt its camera: click the view first, Esc to release
C
next camera (Shift+C: previous)
Space
capture a frame
H
hide or show the interface
M
show where the robot can drive
